## Open for the owner

1. **A link in running text is underlined: the guide's rule** (ui-reviewer's proposal). What it
   is: the rule is now in `frontend/CLAUDE.md` (the review-caught-mistake rule), but not in
   `docs/ui-design-guide.md`. Why it matters: the guide is where reviewers and later screens
   look up rules by ID (comments in Slice 6, the docs page in S2-C21d). Options: (a) a new
   UI-TOK-13 in the guide, by a design change; (b) leave it in `frontend/CLAUDE.md` only.
   Recommendation: (a), a one-line `docs:` change.
2. **DOMPurify's allow-list** (security-reviewer's question; ui-reviewer's coverage gap). What it
   is: DOMPurify runs with its default tags (minus `img`, `style`, `form`), and its URL and `img`
   rules are never the layer that decides, since markdown-it blocks the same cases first, so
   removing them alone fails no test. Why it matters: it's the second layer only if markdown-it
   ever lets HTML through; the CSP is a third. Options: (a) leave it: two layers already stand,
   and no path to harm was found; (b) narrow `ALLOWED_TAGS` to what markdown-it emits and test
   the sanitizer on hand-built HTML, a design change (UI-MD-1). Recommendation: (a).
3. **Bare `www.` autolinks** (checkpoint-reviewer's question). What it is: markdown-it 15 links
   `https://…` and emails, not a bare `www.example.com`. Why it matters: users may expect the
   bare form to link. Options: (a) keep it; (b) turn on `fuzzyLink`, which also links things like
   `file.md` by mistake. Recommendation: (a).
