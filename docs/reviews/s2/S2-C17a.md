## Open for the owner

1. **TD-88's task-list mark** (checkpoint-reviewer's question). What it is: TD-88 said to restyle
   the disabled checkbox; the checkpoint drew a mark in tokens instead (`role="img"`, "Done" or
   "Not done"), since a native checkbox's disabled look can't be reached by our tokens. Why it
   matters: screen readers say "Done, image" rather than "checkbox, checked, unavailable".
   Options: (a) accept the mark; (b) a restyled `input` (`appearance: none`). Recommendation: (a).
2. **Priority as a Select** (ui-reviewer's note). What it is: UI-FRM-12 says radios for three or
   four choices; the create-project type and the role selects are Selects. Options: (a) keep the
   Select and reword UI-FRM-12 (a Select for fixed choices in a dialog's form); (b) radios here,
   and the earlier Selects later. Recommendation: (a), by a design change.
