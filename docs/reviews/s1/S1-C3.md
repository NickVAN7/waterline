## Owner decisions after the review (Oct 7, 2026)

1. **"New password differs from the current one" applies only when users change their own**
   (S1 plan, decision 3, option a): admin resets don't check it, not needed in v1. Design-doc
   §4 and the build plan updated; callers of `password_problems` on the reset path pass
   `same_as_current=False`.
2. **Admins may take account actions on their own account** through the admin path (the
   last-owner and last-system-admin guards still apply), **except changing their own email**,
   which waits for verification emails as the journey decisions set. Design-doc §4 and the
   build plan ("Users") updated.
3. **S1-C8 exports `RESERVED_SLUGS` through the OpenAPI schema** (build plan, Checkpoint 8 row).

S1 plan decision 5 (when TD-2 is fixed) is settled by this checkpoint: S1-C3.
