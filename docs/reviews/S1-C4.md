## Owner decisions after the review (Oct 7, 2026)

1. **Admins can't reset their own password on the admin path** (S2.1): refused, like their own
   email change; they use change password, which asks for the current one. A better path may
   come later. Design-doc §4 and the build plan ("Users", S1-C10) updated.
2. **`include_deleted` needs permission** (S1.1), kept (option B): it enforces design-doc §9's
   trash rule. Ordinary lists never grant it; the trash endpoint grants it only to users with
   restore rights and narrows the rows to what each may restore.
3. **A membership clash on the email-first add path points at `email`** (`already_member`):
   the services in S1-C9 and S1-C12 raise that field error themselves (build plan rows 9 and
   12).
