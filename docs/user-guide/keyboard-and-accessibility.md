Everything in Waterline works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and follows your device's light
or dark setting unless you choose one in [your account](/docs/account#choose-light-or-dark).

## Move around the page

- ++Tab++ moves to the next control, and ++Shift+Tab++ to the one before.
- **Skip to main content** is the first thing ++Tab++ reaches. It jumps past the header and
  sidebar.
- When a new page opens, the focus moves to its title.

## The requirements tree

- ++Right arrow++ opens a branch, and ++Left arrow++ closes it.
- Every drag has another way to do the same: a row's **Move** menu, and the side panel's edge,
  which you can click, or focus and move with ++Left arrow++ and ++Right arrow++.

## Dialogs and panels

- ++Esc++ closes a dialog, a menu, or the side panel.
- If you've changed something, you're asked before it's lost.
