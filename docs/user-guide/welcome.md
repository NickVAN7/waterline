Waterline keeps a project's requirements, their approvals, and the people who work on them in one
place, so you can trace each requirement from the first draft to its sign-off.

## How it's organized

Your company has one **workspace**. Inside it, each **organization** holds its own
**projects**, and each project holds its **requirements**. You see the organizations and projects
you belong to, and nothing else.

## Find your way around

Every page has the same frame:

- **The header** shows the organization you're in. If you belong to more than one, select its
  name to switch. Your name, at the right, opens your account menu, with **Account settings**,
  **Documentation**, and **Sign out**.
- **The sidebar** starts with **My work**, your home page. Below it, the project selector lists the
  organization's projects; once you're in a project, its pages are listed under it.
- **The trail** above a page's title shows where you are. Each step is a link back.

On a narrow screen the sidebar is behind the **Menu** button in the header.
