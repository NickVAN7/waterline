The system status page says whether Waterline and its database are up. You don't need to sign in
to see it, so it's the place to look when something isn't working.

## Check the status

Open the status page from the link on any page-not-found page. It says everything is up, or what's
wrong. Select **Check again** to look again.

> [!TIP]
> If Waterline can't be reached at all, tell your workspace's administrator.
